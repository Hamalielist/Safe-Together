Safety tips for everyday campus life

Pick the habits that fit your routine. You do not need to do everything, just a few things consistently.

Night walks

Before you go

  • Choose well-lit, busy paths even if they are a little longer.
  • Walk with a buddy or ask a friend to stay on a call with you.
  • Share your live location with someone you trust.
  • Keep your phone charged and your earphones at a low volume.

On the way

  • Keep your head up and walk with purpose.
  • If something feels wrong, head to a lit building or a security post.

Online safety

Protect your information

  • Do not post your daily class schedule or exact location in public.
  • Use a strong, unique password for your school account and turn on two-step login.
  • Be careful with links that claim to be from school. Check the sender before you click.
  • Never share your passwords or one-time codes with anyone, even someone who says they are staff.

Dorm and lab safety

Know your space

  • Never prop doors open or let strangers follow you inside.
  • Learn where your nearest exit and fire extinguisher are.
  • Report broken locks, lights, or alarms right away.
  • In labs, follow equipment rules and never work alone late at night.

If you see something, say something

You do not have to handle a problem alone. Tell a staff member, call security, or use our contact form to report a concern. Reports can help others stay safe too.
