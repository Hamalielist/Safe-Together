Three messages to remember

Safety is not about fear. It is about small habits that protect you and the people around you.

👁

Stay aware

Keep your head up and your volume low when you walk. Notice exits, lights, and who is around you.

📱

Stay connected

Charge your phone, share your location with a friend, and tell someone when you will be home.

📢

Speak up

See a broken light or a propped-open door? Report it. Small reports prevent big problems.

Why this campaign matters

A campus feels like home only when people feel safe in it. Safe Together brings students, staff, and security together so that help is easy to find and everyone knows what to do.
