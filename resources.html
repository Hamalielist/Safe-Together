Emergency posts

Look for the blue light posts around campus. Press the button to reach security.

Tip: Learn where the nearest one is.

Listen: the Safe Together alert chime

Text alternative for the audio

A soft, six-second chime of four rising notes that fade out. It has no spoken words. The reminder it stands for: stay on lit, busy paths; keep your phone charged and tell a friend where you are; and if something feels wrong, go to a lit building or call security.
